rclone Pi privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who uses it

rclone Pi is used by one person, the owner of this GitHub account, with their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it touches

When the owner signs in, Google lets rclone read and write the files in the owner's own Google Drive. Files move only between Google Drive and the owner's Raspberry Pi.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or keep data anywhere else.
  • It does not share, sell or send data to anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security, Your connections to third party apps and services.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
